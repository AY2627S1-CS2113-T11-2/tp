# ChatGPat

ChatGPat is a command-line application for NUS students who keep track of their own grades, S/U options and study plan. If you type quickly, it gets you answers faster than updating a spreadsheet.

With ChatGPat, you can:

* record your modules with their MCs and grades, and see your GPA straight away
* try out S/U decisions within your S/U quota, and see how your GPA changes
* find the average grade you need in your remaining MCs to reach a target GPA
* plan your modules by year and semester, filter them, and see a summary of each semester

Your data is saved automatically on your own computer, in a text file that you can back up or edit.
